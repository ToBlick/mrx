## 4. Lessons worth keeping

- **Views:** a "known" coefficient vector of the base (the constant, the all-ones) must be reduced through X^T,
  never rewritten on the view (7355af0: the k=0 test's 37 % error). Sqrt 2 on every orbit pair.
- **Cancelling dots:** the energy-force identity holds to tol |B||Q|, not to tol |dE| (d459adf).
- **Width measure has a floor** (0.028 / 0.023 / 0.019 at 3/5, 1/2, 3/7 on the nested 32^3 state; 0.054 at 16^3):
  quote it as +- on w_meas; never compare measured widths across meshes without it.
- **"Relaxed to the floor" is a claim:** check that the minimum is not the last step and that the last 20 steps
  are within a few per cent of the previous 20. newton_16/24 at step 100 were NOT floored; the 16/24 seed
  experiments got 100 more steps first (`eq16_floor`, `eq24_floor`, step 200).
- **Per-arm resistive references cannot converge.** A heat step removes sheets, not islands.
- **Cancelled jobs still write their outputs.** Delete the stale checkpoint or rename the new one (`_200`, `_q`).
- **The chain count of the seed selection is the spectral cutoff** (m <= n_theta/2: 8/17/28 at 16/24/32), not
  the equilibrium's resonance content.
- The suite on the parity branch is 54/54 in all three configurations (cold 5-8 min each with the XLA cache).
