# The velocity smoothing constant, swept at the publication mesh (2026-09-05)

The smoother is `u = (M_2 + mu L_2)^{-1} M_2 F`, `mu = c h^2` with `h = 1/n_r`
in logical units, so a mode of wavenumber `k` is damped by `1 / (1 + mu k^2)`
and the constant `c` sets the knee in units of the grid. The production value
`c = 0.064` came from one sweep at 8^3 p=3 in August (`mu = 1e-3` at `n_r =
8`; `handoff_2026-08-25_relaxation_prelim.md`, section 37). Tobias: "8,16,8
is a very low resolution to derive anything from."

## Arms

li383 `wout_li383_1.4m.nc`, (16,32,32) p=2, mixed precision (float32 work,
float64 residual, tol 1e-8), L-BFGS m=1, CFL 0.5, 5000 steps in chunks of
500, floor 0; `c` in {0.0064, 0.02, 0.064, 0.2, 0.64} and no smoothing.
`outputs/mu_sweep/<arm>/`.

## What it says

- **Residual per step:** a flat optimum over `c` = 0.02-0.064, both ends of
  the range worse; the order at 2000 steps was already this one. Per wall
  second, 0.02 is the cheapest of the band (the shifted solve's cost grows
  with `mu`), 0.0064 close behind.
- **No smoothing is not a factor 8-16 slower, it stalls:** the order-0 arm's
  chunk-mean residual went 6.3e-4, 6.3e-4, 7.0e-4, 6.5e-4, 5.8e-4, 6.7e-4
  from 2500 to 5000 steps while every smoothed arm kept descending; at
  equal wall time it is a factor 2 behind at 1300 s and three at the end.
- **Helicity drift does not depend on `c`:** -3.7e-8 to -4.3e-8 for every
  smoothed arm, -8.7e-8 without smoothing. Discrete helicity is conserved
  for any velocity (`(v x H, H) = 0`); the drift is the explicit time
  discretisation's and grows with the step, which is why the unsmoothed arm,
  at the smallest `dt` but the roughest velocity, drifts most. The August
  claim of a sharp minimum in `c` was a coarse-mesh artefact.
- **Energy removed decreases with `c`:** the smoothed descent is not the
  steepest in the L2 metric; it trades energy descent for residual descent.
  The differences are 2e-7 on E = 0.5.
- **The fixed point is `c`-independent** (`u` vanishes iff `F` does); the
  end-state pressures agree to the percent (beta_vol 4.28-4.33e-2).

Decision (Tobias): `c = 0.02`, `mrx.relaxation.SMOOTHING_C`, the stepper's
and the driver's default since `8297c83`.
