## 3. The tolerance sets the residual the descent can reach (tol 1e-6)

`h16_p2_tol1e-6` ran 5000 steps like the anchor (2.35e-4 at 5000 against
2.17e-4) and lost the descent in its continuation: from step 5001 on, 40%
of the steps RAISE the energy although the line search predicts a decrease
on every one; around step 8410 the descent cosine falls to 0.000, the
accepted dt collapses from 2.4 to 0.03, the residual climbs from 5e-4 to
1.2e-1 by step 8667, one downhill step there releases 6.4e-6 of energy
(twice the first 5000 steps' total) with the helicity jumping by +1.8e-7,
and the residual returns to 3.4e-4 over the next 1300 steps. Numerical
reconnection, the order-0 floor-study phenomenon, here from the solver
tolerance alone.

The pollution law of the floor study (the direction error of the solves is
tol / resid relative to the force, its energy term 0.1 tol / resid^2 of
the descent) puts the polluted regime at resid < sqrt(0.1 tol) = 3e-4 for
tol 1e-6, which is where the first run ended. For the anchor (tol 1e-8) the
term is 0.03 at 1.5e-4 and 0.07 at the 1.1e-4 the longest continuation
reached; none of the tol 1e-8 / 1e-10 arms has a chunk with rising energy.
So a tolerance buys a residual: about sqrt(tol / 10), 3e-4 at 1e-6, 3e-5 at
1e-8, 3e-6 at 1e-10, and below it the ideal descent stops being ideal. The
1e-8 default is the right one for the residuals these runs reach.

## 4. The float32 underflow (fixed `5887b72`)

The first launch of `h16_p2_tol1e-10` went NaN at step 9 and `h16_p4` at
step 2394, both with every solve and every piece of the step finite when
re-evaluated eagerly from the last finite state (probes
`outputs/sweeps_2026-09/probe_tol1e-10*.py`). A CG instrumented with
`jax.debug.print` inside the compiled step found it: p^T A p and r^T z at
1e-44 (float32 denormals), then r^T z exactly 0, then p^T A p 0 with r^T z
still 1.4e-45, alpha = inf. `refine` cast the float64 residual to float32
as it was; at tol 1e-10 on a right-hand side of 1e-12 that residual is a
1e-22 vector whose squared float32 norms cannot be represented. The inner
solve now receives r / norm(r) and the correction is scaled back (exact for
a linear solve; the scale is positive by the loop condition). 150 single
steps at tol 1e-10 with the instrumented CG: no breakdown; suite 54/54/54
in the three configurations; both arms relaunched and complete without
incident. `docs/source/concepts/precision.md` has the paragraph.
