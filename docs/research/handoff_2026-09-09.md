- `mrx.geometry`: `parse_r_refine` and `radial_knots` (the window spec) are
  gone; `knot_vector(breakpoints, p, periodic)` pads a breakpoint list to the
  full knot vector (clamped: ends repeated `p` times; periodic: the `p` cells
  either side continued by the period, the structure `SplineBasis` builds),
  `parse_knots` reads a comma list. `build_sequence(..., knots=(r, theta,
  zeta))` takes breakpoints per axis (`None` = uniform) and derives that
  axis's `n` (cells + p clamped, cells periodic). The sequence constructor
  keeps its full-knot-vector `knots=` and now checks the periodic count too.
- Angular breakpoints must be uniform: the map's series projection
  (`mrx.gvec`, the circulant collocation/mass row) raises on a non-uniform
  periodic basis (measured: the three-axis smoke run failed there), so
  `build_sequence` rejects non-uniform angular breakpoints with that reason.
  Nothing else in the sequence, the solves or the atoms assumes uniform
  angles (no Fourier symbol anywhere but that projection). Lifting it is
  local: per mode, the moment against the non-uniform basis by quadrature
  and one 1-D mass solve instead of the division by the symbol
  (`series_tensor_coefficients`); not done, and the atoms are untested on
  non-uniform angles.
- `scripts/relax.py`: `--knots-r/-theta/-zeta LIST` replace `--r-refine`;
  `params["knots"]` (three lists or null) and `params["ns"]` (derived) are
  what `poincare_trace.py` and `plot_relaxation.py` read back (`r_refine` is
  gone from the run files; the four shipped `data/tutorials` files updated).
